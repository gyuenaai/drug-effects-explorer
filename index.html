Cannabinoid

Cannabis

Weed · Marijuana · THC
Cannabis acts on CB1/CB2 receptors. High THC strains produce strong psychoactive effects. Often used medically for pain, nausea.
Moderate addiction
Smoked: 2-10m, Edible: 30m-2h
Depressant

Alcohol

Ethanol · Liquor · Beer
One of most used psychoactive substances. CNS depressant that slows brain function. Dose-dependent from disinhibition to coma.
High addiction
10-30 minutes on empty stomach
Stimulant

Nicotine

Tobacco · Vape · Cigarettes
Stimulant that drives tobacco dependence. Creates rapid dopamine surge, but delivery via smoke or vape carries separate harms.
Very High addiction
Smoked/vape: ~20 seconds to brain
Stimulant

Cocaine

Coke · Blow · Snow
Potent stimulant from coca leaf. Blocks dopamine reuptake, causing intense euphoria but short duration.
High addiction
Snorted: 1-5m, smoked (crack): seconds
Opioid

Heroin

H · Smack · Dope
Diacetylmorphine, rapid-acting opioid. Produces powerful analgesia and euphoria with high overdose and addiction risk.
Very High addiction
Injection: ~20s, Smoked: <10s, Snorted: 5-10m
Empathogen

MDMA

Ecstasy · Molly · E
Entactogen releasing serotonin, norepinephrine, dopamine. Promotes empathy and euphoria, but depletes serotonin.
Low-Moderate addiction
30-60m oral
Psychedelic

LSD

Acid · Lucy · Tabs
Classic serotonergic psychedelic, ultra potent microgram dose. Alters perception without direct neurotoxicity.
Very Low addiction
30-90m
Psychedelic

Psilocybin

Magic Mushrooms · Shrooms · Psilocybe
Tryptamine psychedelic in ~200 mushroom species. Prodrug to psilocin, similar to LSD but shorter, more organic headspace.
Very Low addiction
20-60m eaten
Stimulant

Methamphetamine

Meth · Crystal · Ice
Potent amphetamine releasing dopamine massively and lasting. Neurotoxic at high doses, binge pattern.
Extremely High addiction
Smoked/injected: seconds; oral: 20m
Stimulant

Amphetamine / Adderall

Adderall · Speed · Vyvanse
Medical stimulant for ADHD; misused for performance. Less potent/long than meth but similar risks at high doses.
High addiction
Oral: 30-60m
Opioid

Oxycodone

Oxy · Percocet · OxyContin
Semi-synthetic opioid. Effective analgesic with rapid tolerance, dependence, respiratory depression risk.
High addiction
Oral immediate: 20-30m, extended: ~1h
Opioid

Fentanyl

Fetty · Apache · Carfentanil analogue
Synthetic opioid 50-100x morphine potency, microgram dosing. Medical anaesthetic but illicitly mass-fatal overdose driver.
Extremely High addiction
Iv seconds, patch minutes-hours
Depressant

Benzodiazepines

Xanax · Valium · Ativan
GABA-A enhancers, sedative-anxiolytic. Effective short-term but tolerance, dependence, deadly with alcohol/opioids.
High addiction
Oral: 20-60m, alprazolam fast
Dissociative

Ketamine

K · Special K · Ket
Dissociative anaesthetic blocking NMDA. Low dose antidepressant and trippy; high dose K-hole and anesthesia.
Low-Moderate addiction
Snorted 5-15m, injection <5m
Dissociative

PCP

Angel Dust · Phencyclidine · Sherm
Dissociative anesthetic with both stimulant and depressant and psychedelic. Unpredictable aggression and numbness, powerful.
Moderate addiction
Smoked 2-5m, oral 30-60m
Psychedelic

DMT

Dimitri · Spirit Molecule · Ayahuasca core
Endogenous tryptamine, ultra short but breakthrough psychedelic. Smoked induces 5 minute intense visionary.
Very Low addiction
Smoked: seconds, Ayahuasca: 30-60m
Steroid

Anabolic Steroids

Roids · Juice · Test
Synthetic testosterone derivatives building muscle but disrupting hormones and cardiovascular system.
Moderate addiction
Oral days, injectable weeks
Inhalant / Dissociative

Nitrous Oxide

NOS · Whippets · Laughing Gas
Inhaled anaesthetic gas causing 30-60s dissociative euphoria. Low acute risk isolated but B12 depletion and asphyxia risk.
Low-Moderate addiction
Inhaled seconds
Stimulant

Caffeine

Coffee · Energy Drinks · Tea
World's most widely used psychoactive drug. Adenosine receptor blocker that promotes wakefulness and alertness. Very safe at normal doses, but toxic in gram quantities such as pure powder or stacked pills.
Low-Moderate addiction
15-45 minutes
Psychedelic

2C-B

Nexus · Bees · Venus
Synthetic phenethylamine psychedelic developed by Alexander Shulgin. A 5-HT2A agonist that is strongly dose-sensitive: entactogenic and stimulating at low doses, fully psychedelic at higher ones.
Very Low addiction
20-90m oral; faster and more painful if insufflated
Depressant

GHB

G · Liquid Ecstasy · GBL
CNS depressant with an extremely narrow margin between an active dose and overdose. Occurs naturally in the body and is prescribed for narcolepsy; illicitly popular in club and chemsex scenes and notorious for overdose ('G-hole') and drug-facilitated assault.
Moderate-High addiction
10-20 minutes
Inhalant

Poppers

Amyl Nitrite · Alkyl Nitrites · Rush
Alkyl nitrite vapors inhaled for a brief head-rush and smooth-muscle relaxation. Powerful, fast, short-lived blood-vessel dilators popular in clubbing and among men who have sex with men. Low risk inhaled in isolation, but dangerous if swallowed or combined with erectile-dysfunction drugs.
Low addiction
Seconds (inhaled)
Opioid

Morphine

MS Contin · Morph · Miss Emma
Naturally occurring opioid from the poppy and the benchmark against which other opioids are measured. Gold-standard analgesic for severe acute and cancer pain, carrying the classic opioid risks of dependence and respiratory depression.
High addiction
Oral 30-60m; IV within minutes

Educational purpose only

Data disclaimer: Profiles are distilled from public health resources (NIDA, CDC, FDA, EMA). Not comprehensive. Do not use to calculate dose. No safe dose for illicit fentanyl exists. Excludes common OTC analgesics per scope (no Aspirin/Tylenol).
